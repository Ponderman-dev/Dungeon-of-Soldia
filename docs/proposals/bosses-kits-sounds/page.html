Dungeon of Soldia · proposals

Bosses, hero kits & sounds

Three proposals to look over before anything goes into the game: eight bosses with their own trick, a rework so every hero plays differently, and every sound the game needs. Nothing here is built yet. Tell me which parts you like and I'll build them one step at a time.

Proposal 1 · every 10 floors

Eight bosses

One boss every 10 floors (10 to 80). Each one has ONE trick that asks you to use a different part of the game: tapping a target, timing a skill, picking an element, switching targets. Bosses show a big health bar at the top and a red cast bar before their big moves. Pixel designs are drafts in the game's style (they can be redrawn in the Studio later).

Floor 90 and 100 (later): floor 90 could be a "gauntlet" (two earlier bosses together, stronger), and floor 100 the super boss. I'd design those once the first bosses feel right.

Proposal 2 · rework

Hero kits that feel different

Right now many heroes share the same pieces: 7 of 10 attack skills hit every enemy, three heroes have an "every 4th attack" counter, three give squad shields, two have channels. The rework gives each hero ONE signature mechanic nobody else has, and keeps what already works (perks, most passives, the art).

Proposal 3 · audio

Every sound effect

The full list, grouped by where you hear it. The ▶ buttons play a rough 8-bit sketch made right in your browser, only to give the idea; the real sounds would be made properly (retro "chiptune" style fits the pixel art).
