Soldia hit effects

Every hit, status, item proc and hero skill as a looping pixel animation, using the new sprites. Each one is drawn from small pixels and simple shapes (rings, sparks, bolts, numbers), so they stay crisp at game size and can be tuned later.

Colour = damage typeMelee white, arrows pale yellow, fire orange, ice light blue, electric yellow, dark purple, holy gold, bleed red, poison green. Numbers use the same colour.
Size = importanceNormal hits are small and quick. Item procs are medium. Crits get a star and a shake. Skills are the biggest: they fill the screen.
Statuses loop on the unitA status is a small looping effect on the character (stars, ice, flames, bubbles) plus its tag by the health bar, so you can read a fight without reading text.
Everything stays pixelNo smooth glows or blurs: rings and sparks are drawn pixel by pixel, matching the sprites.

Hits

What every single hit looks like, by damage type, plus crits, misses, blocks, shields, heals and deaths.

Statuses

Buffs and debuffs that sit on a unit. These loop for as long as the status lasts.

Item procs

The moments when an item goes off. Each item has its own shape and colour.

Hero skills

The big moments. These come on top of the skill banner and the hero's own skill pose.

Simplified version (suggestion)

The same game, with fewer one-off effects. Five ideas: a small kit of pieces that every effect is built from; item procs show the item's icon instead of a custom effect; minor debuffs share one look and the tag says which; only the most important status loops on a unit; one added-up damage-over-time number per second. Hero skills and a handful of special items stay exactly as in the full version above.

Status icons (new)

Like Risk of Rain 2: every status gets its own small pixel icon, in a row over the health bar, with the number of stacks in the corner. Each icon has its own shape (not a coloured square) and a dark outline that follows the shape. Big on the left, game size on the right.

Stress test: 5 enemies, 5 debuffs each

The worst case: five enemies, every one carrying five debuffs at the same time. Same fight drawn three ways, shown bigger so you can judge it at a glance.
