# New items batch 1 (FINAL, all decided with the user in session 27, NOT built yet)

21 new items (92 -> 113). Rule (item-decisions.md): every extra copy adds a real +5-10% or more.
"Holder" = the hero holding the item.

## Bomb family (rare; existing Fire Bombs is the fire member, unchanged)
All like Fire Bombs: 8% chance per attack (+8% per copy) to throw a 300% bomb of their element + its debuff.
1. Ice Bomb: + Chill.
2. Electric Bomb: + 1s shock-stun.
3. Dark Bomb: + enemy deals -15% damage for 4s.
4. Poison Bombs: + 3 poison stacks.
5. Bombardier's Barrel (legendary): when a bomb goes off, 25% chance (+5% per copy) to throw 2-4 more bombs,
   each a RANDOM pick from the bomb types the holder has. +100% bomb damage (+100% per copy).

## Badges and damage bonuses
6. Hunter's Badge (common): +20% damage to elites (+20% per copy).
7. Elite's Badge (rare): +20% damage to bosses (+20% per copy).
8. Better Odds (epic): +2.5% damage per enemy ALIVE right now (+2.5% per copy).
9. Swarm Swatter (common): +25% damage to fodder (+12% per copy).

## Legendaries
10. Frozen Demon Head: +50% ice damage (+35% per copy). Stores the holder's ice damage; when it holds 8x the holder's
    attack, Ice Spikes hit a random enemy (stronger ones first) for 700% (+150% per copy) + 5s Freeze.
    The holder's fire/electric/dark damage is REMOVED (other charms do nothing, a Mage loses her other elements).
11. Euclid of Elements: a hit with 2+ different elements (two different charms, Chill Band, Magma Core, element
    skills; physical is not an element) has 15% chance (+5% per copy) to cast 3 more times at 60% (+20% per copy).
12. Sharpest Blade: hits with NO element (any charm rider turns it off; the Mage can't use it): 6% chance (+3% per
    copy) to strike the heart for 4.5x damage (+0.5x per copy) + 2.5s stun.

## Commons
13. Life Crystal: the holder's kills (?) 15% chance (+5% per copy) to drop a green orb: heals heroes NEAR the dead enemy 5% max health.
14. Emergency Buckler: the holder's kills give the holder a 6% max-health shield (+3% per copy) that fades over 4s.
15. Opening Horn: first 4s of each floor +30% attack speed (+12% per copy).
16. Sweeping Strap (melee): basic attacks also hit enemies within ~60px of the target for 15% (+8% per copy).
17. Quick Fingers: the holder's kills cut the holder's skill cooldowns by 0.5s (+0.3s per copy).

## Rares and epic
18. Frost Mantle (rare): hit by melee: 30% chance (+5% per copy) to chill the attacker.
19. Kinetic Coil (rare): each dodge stores a charge (max 3, kept until used, reset each floor); the next attack deals
    +40% electric per charge (+20% per charge per copy). Meant for dodgy heroes.
20. Rally Flag (rare): once per floor per flag holder, when a hero drops under 30%: every hero gets a 12% max-health
    shield (+6% per copy). Shields don't stack: a new rally replaces the old one.
21. Reaper's Bell (epic): any kill by the holder (attack or skill) explodes for 18% of the enemy's max health
    (+8% per copy) as dark damage nearby. Explosions can chain forever (exception to the item-damage rule).
