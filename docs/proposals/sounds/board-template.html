Finding good sounds in a huge library

Search by word, not by folderOn your computer, search the bundle for words like sword, impact, whoosh, magic, fire, ice, zap, electric, coin, door, footstep, gong, monster, ui.
Keep them shortFight sounds should be under 1 second. Skip long ambience and music beds for now: they are big and we need few of them.
Try before sendingUse "Try my own" on any card above to hear a file in the right place, at the game's volume, next to the other options.
Send a small zipPick 3 to 10 files per moment, put them in a zip (under about 100 MB) and attach it in chat. I'll trim the silence, make them small and mono, match their loudness and add them as new options here.
Name them by momentRename files like hitMelee - sword1.wav so I know where each one goes. Big WAV files are fine.
