# Rewards

Reward screen, drop roll, potion, who-gets-it.
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **Rewards:** after every floor clear a full-screen reward scene (RewardScene, layout from the user's wireframes) shows 3 items. Drop roll (`rollChoices()` in rewards.js): each card first rolls a RARITY with odds by floor (`rewards.json` `rarityOdds`: floors 1-10 62/29/7.5/1.5, 11-30 52/32/13/3, 31+ 42/34/19/5 common/rare/epic/legendary), then an item of that rarity by its `dropWeight` (default 100). An item with `needs` (e.g. Eagle Eye needs "ranged") gets x0.25 weight while no living hero/item provides it (`squadSources()`: hero damageType, hero `provides`, item `provides`). `rarities.json` only holds labels and colours, then a "Who gets it?" screen (hero cards, 2 per row with the third centred, with before > after stats, then a Give button). Only items for now; skill books come next. Rarities: common (grey), rare (blue), epic (purple), legendary (gold). Items: `items.json` (effects: `stat` + `percent` or `flat`; `damageBonus` + `percent`; or `special` + `percent`; `scope` self or squad; optional `maxStacks`). HERO HUD (user's choice): no name/level/perk text under the skill squares; the hero's LEVEL shows as a small yellow "Lv3" just left of its health bar (`view.lvl`, updated on level-up); its ITEMS show UNDER its skill squares as small framed item icons at scale 1 (20x20, the frame tells the rarity, `drawDots()`): one per kind of item, a white number when it has several copies, 5 per row, 2 rows, more kinds = "+N" in the 10th spot; hidden with the skill squares during floor changes, dim when the hero is dead. Only items whose effects work today are in the pool (no magic/mana items yet).
- **Shared Potion:** very rare (`potionChance` 5% per reward screen, only if a living hero is hurt) replaces one of the 3 cards; picking it heals every living hero by `healPercent` (25%) and skips the hero-choice step.

The door opens first (`transition.clearPauseMs` 900), then the sheet comes up.
