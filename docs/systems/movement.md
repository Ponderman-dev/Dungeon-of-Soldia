# Movement

Where fighters stand and how they walk (rules side, not the scene).
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **MOVEMENT (rebuilt, Warcraft-3 style, user's drawing):** positions live in the RULES, not the scene. Every fighter has `x, y` (its feet, screen pixels) once `BattleState.place()` was called (the scene does it in `startFighting()`; tests/bot use `placeLines()` with `movement.layout`). Every update (`BattleState.move()`): a MELEE fighter walks straight at its target at `movement.walkSpeedPxPerSec` (170) until the target is inside its reach oval (`reachX` 64 sideways, `reachY` 94 up/down), then swings; both sides walk so two melee fighters meet in the middle, face to face. RANGED fighters stay where they are and shoot. Fighters closer than the `separateX/Y` oval push apart (`separatePushPerSec`; ranged ones hold their ground), so a crowd spreads around a target instead of stacking. Pushing is GENTLE (user: "a little push is fine", crowds used to slide into the walls): a fighter standing still gives way only `settledPushShare` (0.3) as much as a walking one, two standing fighters drift apart at `settledPairPush` (0.4) of the speed, and within `wallSoftPx` (60) of a side wall a fighter is hardly pushed further towards it (test `crowd.test.mjs`). Feared = walks back to its own spot; knocked back = pushed `knockbackPx` towards its own side over `knockbackMs`; stunned/frozen or mid-swing = stands still. Nobody can move faster than walking + pushing, so no teleporting or sliding. The scene only DRAWS: `followPositions()` puts each figure at its position every frame, with a hop per step while `moving` (`attackMotion.hopHeight` / `hopLengthPx`, rhythm from `walked`); the hop only lifts at its OWN walking pace (`u.speed`, 0 when it is only shoved; full hop at `hopFullSpeedShare` of walk speed, rises over `hopStartMs`, sinks over `hopEaseMs`), so a fighter jostled in a crowd stays on the ground (bug fixed: Grukk hung mid-hop = 'floating'); lower heroes are drawn in front. FACING: every fighter is mirrored left/right to look at whoever it fights (its swing target, its opponent, or the last thing it shot at; `Character.face()`, the art looks the way its weapon part points); heroes look the normal way again after a win. HEALTH BARS are always drawn on top of every character: enemy bars/tags slide in with their floor, then leave the floor container in `startFighting()` and get the `heroBar` depth. Targets: enemies keep their opponent, a new one is the NEAREST hero by real distance (`nearest()`); melee heroes likewise; ranged heroes pick at random; tap-focus wins. After a win heroes walk back to their slots at walking speed. BIG fighters (bosses, `size` from the sprite width / `normalBodyPx`): the push-apart oval grows with both sizes, and so does REACH (a hero hits a boss from its edge, a boss swings from its own edge; before 2026-10-19 only the target's size counted, so bosses walked into the hero, were shoved out and walked back in = overlap + jitter; tests in `movement.test.mjs`). Without positions (some rule tests) nobody moves and melee can always hit. Tests: `movement.test.mjs` (walk, meet, speed, ranged stay, no teleport, no stacking, retarget, fear, knockback, targets).
- **MINIONS MAKE WAY, BOSSES HOLD (user, 2026-10-20, floor 40 Brood Mother screenshot: a crowd of skeletons/imps kept the Berserker away and shoved the boss all over the map):** in the push-apart, a minion always gives way fully to a non-minion (hero or enemy) and the non-minion is not pushed at all, so minions never block a hero or shove an enemy; a fighter `bigHoldRatio` (1.5) times bigger than the other (a boss vs anything normal-sized) is not pushed by it (`share()` in `move()`). PASS THROUGH (user, same day): a minion that is STANDING STILL does not collide with anyone who is walking (heroes, enemies, other minions walk straight through it, `ghost` in move()); once both stand still they drift apart as usual (the minion moves, see above). A boss next to its own OBJECT (Brood Mother egg sacs) used to be shoved by it (both "hold" = fell back to both move); now neither budges. Shove per enemy per fight (scratch sim, floor 30): normal enemies 32px -> 13px (with 10 minions 84px -> 3px), Brood Mother 114-169px -> 0px. Measured (Brood Mother floor 40 + 10 minions, scratch sim): Berserker out of reach 63% -> 0% of the time; a boss standing still moves 0px (was 14-46px). The boss still WALKS after heroes on its own (~100px a fight). Smart curve: median 40 -> 41; melee heroes did better (Berserker avg floor 43 -> 50, Knight 42 -> 48) and so did the Necromancer (62 -> 88: its minions now reach the fight; watch it). Tests in movement.test.mjs.
- **PARKED (user, 2026-10-20):** 'bosses should NEVER be shoved, they do the shoving'. Now: a boss is not pushed by anything less than `bigHoldRatio` (1.5x) its size and pushes those out of its way; it can still be pushed by another big body (e.g. twin bosses) and by knockback. To do later: make bosses (`def.boss`) never budge at all.
- **MELEE STEP-IN (user, 2026-10-20: 'the knight swings without the feel of contact'):** every melee BASIC attack steps in to its target (`BattleScene.stepIn()`, combat.json `attackMotion.meleeStep`): fighters stand at reach, so the view moves towards the target (distance minus `contactPx` x the body sizes, at most `maxPx` 40), arrives exactly at the hit (hitInMs), holds `holdMs`, steps back in `backMs`. Heroes, minions and enemies (`sides`). View offsets `stepX/stepY` (separate from the leap offsets); the rules position does not change.
- **MOVE ORDERS (user's pick, built session 38; asked for in session 30 to dodge the Frost Warden's Ice Spikes):** DRAG a hero onto the empty floor = only that hero walks there (white arrow + small white ring while dragging; red arrow = over an enemy = drag order as before); a short TAP on the empty fight floor = the whole squad walks there side by side (`moveOrders.gatherSpacingPx` 54 apart, in squad order; a tap right next to an enemy still focuses it). While walking a hero does NOT attack (an attack it had started is dropped, `attackCancel` reason `move`; its swing timer waits so it attacks on arrival); on arrival (within `arrivePx`) it goes straight back to fighting: a melee hero walks back to its target, a ranged one shoots from its new spot. A move order replaces that hero's drag order. Fear and knockback still win while they last, a skill wind-up finishes first; gives up after `maxMs` (stuck in a crowd). Ends with the floor and with death. Rules: `BattleState.setMove(heroUid, x, y, events)` / `moveSquad(x, y, events)` / `unit.walkTo` (checked in `move()`), numbers in combat.json `moveOrders`. Scene: `floorTap()` / `onFloor()` / `orderMove()` (white ring under each destination + a tap ripple, 'Moving!' for a single hero), drag release in `endOrderDrag()`. The balance bot never uses them. Tests: `move-orders.test.mjs`.
- **TAP FIX (user: 'the enemy circle is too small, I tap the floor by accident and the heroes keep walking instead of
  attacking'):** (1) a floor tap within `moveOrders.enemyTapRadiusPx` (70) of an enemy's middle focuses the NEAREST
  enemy instead of moving the squad (orders.js `floorTap` -> `enemyAt(x, y, minReach)`, red ring + tap sound); drags
  keep the old 40. (2) `setFocus()` on a living enemy clears EVERY hero's `walkTo`, `setOrder()` clears that hero's:
  an attack command always beats a walk. (3) no jostling for the spot: `arrivePx` 4 -> 10, and a hero that has not got
  `stuckPx` closer for `stuckMs` (0.4s) counts as arrived (`walkTo.best` / `.stall`); `maxMs` 4000 -> 3000.
  STRESS RUN (real mouse taps with finger error, 20 floors) found the bigger cause: a hero's touch box (108 x ~96 px,
  for dragging) lies over the enemy it fights and SWALLOWED 4 in 10 dead-centre taps on that enemy. Now a press on a
  hero that travels less than `moveOrders.dragMinPx` (24) is a TAP: `tapEnemy(x, y)` focuses the nearest enemy in the
  tap circle (orders.js `endOrderDrag`); a tap on an enemy's body also goes to the NEAREST enemy, not the one drawn on
  top; near misses above the top row count too. Result (finger error ~12 / ~22 px): right enemy 80% -> 99% / 65% ->
  95%, 'nothing happens' 17-21% -> 0%, squad walks by accident 5% -> 0.2%.
- **BLOCKED MELEE HEROES (same complaint, second cause):** a melee hero sent at an enemy behind a crowd (tap focus, drag
  order) pushed against the front line and did not attack for up to 10s. Now `move()` tracks `u.chase` (target, closest
  distance, stall time); not `movement.blockedPx` closer for `movement.blockedMs` (0.6s) = blocked, and the attack loop
  uses `blockedTarget()`: the nearest enemy IN REACH, swing by swing, until the way is free (the real target is kept;
  taunt is never overruled; skills still aim at the focus). Worst case 10.1s -> 4.0s without an attack. Tests:
  `move-orders.test.mjs`. Smart curve after the change: bosses 20 / 40 / 60 at 89% / 78% / 36% (the usual range).
- **NO SHOVING (user, session 45: 'when units collide they get pushed more and more until they stand in a very bad
  spot; the unit still tries to move past'):** cause: a walker kept walking straight at its target and leaned on
  whoever stood in between (the stander took 23% of every push, for as long as the walker kept trying). Now
  (`movement.walkersYield`, false = the old way): (1) a fighter that STANDS (swinging, waiting, stunned) is a wall for
  one that WALKS: `share()` gives the stander 0, the walker is put back outside the body at once (never wades in).
  (2) GO AROUND: the walking it lost becomes a sidestep along the body, towards the side its goal lies (head-on =
  towards the middle of the floor); it keeps that side for `sidestepKeepMs` (`u.sidestep`) so it does not dither.
  (3) WAIT: an enemy or minion that got no closer for `giveUpMs` (1.5s) stops and waits `waitMs` (1.2s, `u.wait`; it
  stands like everyone else), then tries the other way round (an enemy also re-picks the nearest hero). Heroes do not
  wait: they hit what is in reach (`blockedTarget`). Unchanged: two walkers push each other, two standers drift apart
  gently, knockback, minions make way, bosses hold. A walker held up by a body has `speed` = what it really moved (no
  hopping on the spot). A MOVE ORDER onto a spot inside a standing fighter's body now ends next to it.
  MEASURED (`scripts/dev/push-drift.mjs <repo>`, 30s fights, nobody dies; px a fighter is moved while not walking):
  vs 6 enemies 11-13 -> 0, vs 8 melee 51-56 -> 7-10, vs a crowd of 12: 222 -> 21-31. Side effect: in a crowd the back
  row waits for room (swings per enemy in 30s: 8 melee 17.1 -> 15.2-16.0, crowd of 12 16.1-16.6 -> 13.2-14.8).
  Smart curve 360 runs: median 40 -> 40, mean 44 -> 46, bosses 20 / 40 / 60 at 88% / 83% / 33% (was 89 / 78 / 36).
  About 1 crowd fight in 10 still ends with ~15px of push per fighter (old way ~88px). Tests: `movement.test.mjs` (last
  two blocks; the crowd test uses fixed seeds 1, 8, 12, 23, 31, where 8 and 23 are such busier fights, and also
  seeds Math.random). NOT yet looked at by eye in a real fight on screen.
