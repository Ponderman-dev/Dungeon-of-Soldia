# Movement

Where fighters stand and how they walk (rules side, not the scene).
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **MOVEMENT (rebuilt, Warcraft-3 style, user's drawing):** positions live in the RULES, not the scene. Every fighter has `x, y` (its feet, screen pixels) once `BattleState.place()` was called (the scene does it in `startFighting()`; tests/bot use `placeLines()` with `movement.layout`). Every update (`BattleState.move()`): a MELEE fighter walks straight at its target at `movement.walkSpeedPxPerSec` (170) until the target is inside its reach oval (`reachX` 64 sideways, `reachY` 94 up/down), then swings; both sides walk so two melee fighters meet in the middle, face to face. RANGED fighters stay where they are and shoot. Fighters closer than the `separateX/Y` oval push apart (`separatePushPerSec`; ranged ones hold their ground), so a crowd spreads around a target instead of stacking. Pushing is GENTLE (user: "a little push is fine", crowds used to slide into the walls): a fighter standing still gives way only `settledPushShare` (0.3) as much as a walking one, two standing fighters drift apart at `settledPairPush` (0.4) of the speed, and within `wallSoftPx` (60) of a side wall a fighter is hardly pushed further towards it (test `crowd.test.mjs`). Feared = walks back to its own spot; knocked back = pushed `knockbackPx` towards its own side over `knockbackMs`; stunned/frozen or mid-swing = stands still. Nobody can move faster than walking + pushing, so no teleporting or sliding. The scene only DRAWS: `followPositions()` puts each figure at its position every frame, with a hop per step while `moving` (`attackMotion.hopHeight` / `hopLengthPx`, rhythm from `walked`); the hop only lifts at its OWN walking pace (`u.speed`, 0 when it is only shoved; full hop at `hopFullSpeedShare` of walk speed, rises over `hopStartMs`, sinks over `hopEaseMs`), so a fighter jostled in a crowd stays on the ground (bug fixed: Grukk hung mid-hop = 'floating'); lower heroes are drawn in front. FACING: every fighter is mirrored left/right to look at whoever it fights (its swing target, its opponent, or the last thing it shot at; `Character.face()`, the art looks the way its weapon part points); heroes look the normal way again after a win. HEALTH BARS are always drawn on top of every character: enemy bars/tags slide in with their floor, then leave the floor container in `startFighting()` and get the `heroBar` depth. Targets: enemies keep their opponent, a new one is the NEAREST hero by real distance (`nearest()`); melee heroes likewise; ranged heroes pick at random; tap-focus wins. After a win heroes walk back to their slots at walking speed. BIG fighters (bosses, `size` from the sprite width / `normalBodyPx`): the push-apart oval grows with both sizes, and so does REACH (a hero hits a boss from its edge, a boss swings from its own edge; before 2026-10-19 only the target's size counted, so bosses walked into the hero, were shoved out and walked back in = overlap + jitter; tests in `movement.test.mjs`). Without positions (some rule tests) nobody moves and melee can always hit. Tests: `movement.test.mjs` (walk, meet, speed, ranged stay, no teleport, no stacking, retarget, fear, knockback, targets).
- **MINIONS MAKE WAY, BOSSES HOLD (user, 2026-10-20, floor 40 Brood Mother screenshot: a crowd of skeletons/imps kept the Berserker away and shoved the boss all over the map):** in the push-apart, a minion always gives way fully to a non-minion (hero or enemy) and the non-minion is not pushed at all, so minions never block a hero or shove an enemy; a fighter `bigHoldRatio` (1.5) times bigger than the other (a boss vs anything normal-sized) is not pushed by it (`share()` in `move()`). Measured (Brood Mother floor 40 + 10 minions, scratch sim): Berserker out of reach 63% -> 0% of the time; a boss standing still moves 0px (was 14-46px). The boss still WALKS after heroes on its own (~100px a fight). Smart curve: median 40 -> 41; melee heroes did better (Berserker avg floor 43 -> 50, Knight 42 -> 48) and so did the Necromancer (62 -> 88: its minions now reach the fight; watch it). Tests in movement.test.mjs.
