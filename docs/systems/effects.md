# Hit, item and skill effects

The visual effect recipes (no Phaser) and how the scene draws them.
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **HIT EFFECTS (user's choice: the FULL version, every damage type its own hit):** `src/systems/hitFx.js` (no Phaser, character pixels = world / CHAR_SCALE): `projectileFx(type, start, head, t)` = the shot while it flies (ranged arrow, fire bolt with a flickering tail, spinning ice shard, flickering lightning bolt, dark orb with a smoky tail, holy mote), `impactFx(type, q, u, t, seed, from)` = where it lands, on the side facing the attacker (melee slash + sparks, arrow sparks, fire ring + falling sparks + orange flash, ice frost star + pale flash, electric bolt + sparks + flicker/twitch, dark imploding ring + rising wisps, holy light pillar + floor ring), `critFx()` = crossing gold/white slashes + a star of rays (+ a tiny camera shake). Durations `IMPACT_MS` / `CRIT_MS`. Scene: `shootArrow()` keeps an EMPTY container for the flight position (`arrow.kind` = the attacker's damage type), `drawHitFx()` (called from `drawStatusFx()`, own Graphics at depth popup-1) draws shots + impacts every frame, `addImpact()` from the `attack` event (basic attacks and item hits; skill hits keep their own look for now); a hit's colour flash (`v.hitTint`) wins over the status tint for that frame. Damage numbers unchanged. OTHER HIT MOMENTS (same file, `MOMENT_MS`, `BattleScene.addMoment(kind, view, attacker)`): MISS = the target side-steps away from the attacker and back with grey speed lines (`dodgeFx`), BLOCK/parry = a bright shield shape flashes on the hit side + sparks (`blockFx`), SHIELD SOAK = the bubble lights up + a ripple where it was hit (`soakFx`), SHIELD BREAK = blue shards fly out and fall (`shieldBreakFx`, plus the old popup + shake), HEAL = green motes and plus signs rise + a green glow (`healFx`, at most one every 0.45s per fighter), ENEMY/MINION DEATH = a white flash, it squashes flat into the floor (scaleY tween), a grey pixel puff (`deathPuffFx`, a fixed-position 'puff' entry) and the view is removed after 380ms. Tests: `hit-fx.test.mjs`. One-frame sideways nudges (twitch, side-step, shake) go through `BattleScene.nudge()`: several in one frame never add up past the biggest one, and during hit-stop positions are still reset every frame (they used to pile up and slide enemies away). Skill name banners take the first free row (`bannerRows`) so two at once don't overlap; level-up pops as two short lines. Status/hit tints are cached as textures in 10% steps (`Character.setStatusLook`). Browser checks: screenshots right after a clock step can show the frame before; to inspect an effect, pin it (long `ms`, fixed progress) and screenshot.
- **ITEM EFFECTS (user's choice: FULL effect per item AND the item's icon pops over the holder):** every item `proc` event now carries `itemId` (+ `target` / `at` / `from` / `to` where useful; BattleState only adds these fields, no rule changes; also a `lifesteal` event `{unit, from}` and `thorns.holder`). Scene: a proc with an item id calls `itemProc()`: `iconPop()` (the framed 20x20 item icon at scale 2 (user: smaller; `iconPop.scale`) jumps up over the holder's bar with a white ring, holds, floats up and fades; several pops stand side by side; settings in `src/data/itemFx.json` `iconPop`) INSTEAD of the text label (passive procs and non-item moments keep their text), then the item's own effect if `itemFx.json` names one (`effect`: battleCry = shout ring + speed lines on the squad (War Banner, Jolly Banner; Bloodlust Mask in green), shatter (Shatter Hammer, + shake), knifeBurst (Thousand Cuts, where the enemy died), plague (green wisps to the next enemies), thunderbolt (frost ring), aegisBlast (Spiked Aegis spikes)). Drawn from their own events: Fire Bombs `bomb` projectile (arc + explosion), `lightning` (Static Crystal and Chain Lightning: flickering pixel bolt + sparks), `greenfire` (Grimoire), thorns spikes on the holder, lifesteal drops flying back into the hero, Excalibur tornado = a pixel funnel (`tornadoFx`, the tornado container only carries the position). Recipes in `src/systems/itemFx.js` (no Phaser, `ITEM_MS`), timed by `BattleScene.addFx(ms, fn)` / `fxList` (drawn in `drawHitFx()`), helpers `fxPoint()` / `fxBody()`. `noPop: true` in itemFx.json = no icon pop and no proc sound (user: frequent simple procs: Fire Arrows, Quiver of Plenty, Metronome, and the 8 debuff procs: Venom Fang, Ember Charm, Rusty Nails, Hex Doll, Serrated Edge, Dread Bell, Thousand Cuts, Provoker's Horn; the knife burst still plays). Fire Arrows shoot a real burning arrow (`projectileFx("firearrow")` in hitFx.js: wooden shaft, feathers, a ball of fire on the head with flickering tongues, trailing embers), not a bomb. ITEM NOTES round 1 (user's review page notes, applied): Flame Spirit = big dark swaying fireballs with eyes (projectile `spiritfire`, `itemFx.spiritFireFx`), Grimoire fireball and Moonlight crescent much bigger, War Banner's battleCry is a SHOUT (sound arcs from the head; shared with Jolly Banner / Bloodlust Mask), Frozen Thunderbolt crackles, Spirit Axe = a big wheel spin round the holder, Soul Lantern = glowing green lines swirl round the holder on every kill (`soulFeed` event, `soulLanternFx`), Ghastly Fire = ONE bigger purple fire hit (`ghastlyDraws`; its extra dark hit shows no impact, `attack.ghastly`), Fire Heart's shield = status look `fireShield` (red + flames), Heart of the Beast holder = ORANGE regen `beastRegen` during fights (replaces the green regenBoost look), Mirror Buckler pops 'Reflected!' (itemFx.json `text`), the block flash is bigger (also the Samurai parry). Items without a listed effect just pop their icon (their hits already show the normal hit of their damage type). Also: Sucker Punch (red/yellow impact star on the first hit), Executioner's Axe (red chop through the enemy), Gambler's Dice (a die tumbles above the enemy after a crit), Mimic Chest (a chest pops out beside the holder and chomps); `itemDamageBonus()` notes the items that boosted a hit in `this.bonusItems` and `strike()` turns them into proc events (no rule change). Tests: `item-fx.test.mjs`.
- **SKILL EFFECTS (user's choice: the FULL version, every skill its own effect):** `src/systems/skillFx.js` (no Phaser, character pixels). CAST recipes (`castFx(name, q, c, t)`, `CAST_MS`; c = live bodies of caster / targets / squad / enemies; may return `tints` that colour fighters for a moment): groundPound (dust shockwave along the floor), rallyCry (green ring + a little blue shield over each hero), smokeBomb (grey smoke over every enemy's head), huntersMark (red reticle drops on the target and snaps tight), savageLeap (red impact star + dust where he lands), warCry (red rings, squad glows red, enemies sag grey), windCutter (one white crescent sweeps across the enemy line), focusStance (gold ring closes in + petals + gold edge), frostNova (ice rings from the middle of the enemies, they go icy), arcaneWard (spinning rune circle under the squad), divineShield (golden dome drops over the ally), boneWard (bones orbit each target), ballad (green notes drift up). Which skill plays which: `src/data/skillFx.json` (skill id, a rotation spell = 'skillId:SHORT'); a skill with its own effect skips the plain coloured rings (banner, caster flash, shake and hit-stop stay). SHOT recipes (`shotFx(kind, q, a, b, t, seed, color)`, `SHOT_MS`, from `showProjectile()` / `showFlask()`, aimed at where the target is NOW): knife (spinning), rain (arrows drop from high above + dust), note (pink note), fireball (big fireball with a flame trail, then an explosion), darkbolt (spiralling skull bolt), holybolt (Smite: a holy pillar crashes down), flask (lobbed flask splashing in its colour); crescent = nothing (the Wind Cutter cast sweep covers it); Chain Lightning uses itemFx lightning. `BattleScene.playSkillFx()`. Tests: `skill-fx.test.mjs`.

- **KNIGHT 3-BEAT SKILL LOOK (2026-10-18, user-approved demo https://claude.ai/artifact/NySVH2UUozwNNSYuky9xBN, source docs/proposals/skill-fx):** calm on purpose (user: 'don't interrupt the flow', 'no geometric shapes'). CHARGE = `knightCharge` recipe during the wind-up (combat.json `skillFx.charge` maps skill id -> recipe; `BattleScene.playCharge()`, stops if the wind-up is cut): steel-blue pulsing tint, specks pulled in, glow at the feet, weapon glint. RELEASE = the caster flashes in its hero colour (`skillFx.name.heroColors`) + the usual lunge/hop, shake and hit-stop. IMPACT = `shieldBash` (his shield's pixel print on the target, steel chips, dust) and `challenge` (dust, 3 shout waves towards the enemies, red flash on the taunted, a blue shield pops beside him). Knight colours `KNIGHT` + helpers `shieldPx/dustPx/shoutPx` in skillFx.js. Other heroes: same pattern, 2-3 per session (ideas: Berserker red splatter, Rogue cyan streaks, Cleric gold light, Necro green-purple smoke).
  Round 2 (user tested): Defense Bash got an AGGRESSIVE DASH (combat.json `skillFx.dash.defense_bash`, `BattleScene.dashAt()`: he shoots at the target from wherever he stands, steel streaks behind him, the target is knocked back, small shake at the hit; replaces the small lunge), the shield print is 2x bigger and lands when the dash hits (`c.hitMs`). Challenge: bigger, brighter shout waves (white, 3px first wave), bigger dust, 2x shield pop, a brighter flash on him.
- **ROGUE 3-BEAT LOOK (2026-10-18, built straight in the game, no demo page; same rules: calm, real shapes, name in caps, cyan `ROGUE` colours):** CHARGE `rogueCharge` (cyan flicker getting faster, specks streak in from the sides, knife glints left/right). MULTISTRIKE: aggressive dash in and she STAYS for the 5 hits (`skillFx.dash.multistrike`, holdMs 640, no knockback), `rogueDash` streaks at the cast, then a PER-WAVE look (`skillFx.wave.multistrike` -> `rogueSlash`, `BattleScene.playWaveFx()`, replaces the yellow wave ring): a cyan knife slash each hit (alternating), blood drops, a tiny shake, the hit enemy jitters side to side (`targetShakePx`); the last hit = a big X + a bigger shake. SMOKE BOMB (`smokeBomb` rewritten): a little black bomb with a lit fuse arcs over the enemies, a white pop, a big grey smoke cloud on each enemy, the squad flickers cyan (+evasion).
