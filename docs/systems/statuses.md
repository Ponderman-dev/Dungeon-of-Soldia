# Statuses, shields, taunt

Every status, DoTs, status icons and body effects; shields/invulnerable (D1); taunt (D2).
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **Statuses** (rules + tags in `statuses.json`, passed to BattleState as `statusDefs`): stun (can't attack or cast), freeze (same, ice), CHILL (`stacking`: each stack -10% attack speed, at 5 stacks the unit FREEZES for 1.5s and the chill is used up; plain Slow no longer exists), SHOCK (-20% attack speed and 10% chance per attack to fumble = 0.3s stun; refreshes), FEAR (can't attack), KNOCKBACK (`instant`: attack timer restarts, nothing lasting), TAUNT (must attack the unit that taunted it while it lives), SILENCE (can't cast skills; for later enemy skills), stat debuffs (refresh per source, different sources ADD UP; worked out in combat.js `computeDamage`): WEAKEN (-20% attack), ARMOR BREAK (-15 defense, can go below 0 = more damage), CURSE (-15 resist), BLIND (25% of attacks miss), MARK (+15% damage taken, also from damage over time), buff (temporary stat `mods`, same shape as item effects). DAMAGE OVER TIME (`dot`: bleed = physical and ignores defense, burn = fire and ticks every 0.5s, poison = dark): every application adds a STACK with NO limit, each stack has its own timer, all stacks of one kind tick together every `tickMs` as one number (`damageMultiplier` x caster attack per stack, ignores dodge/crit). Other statuses: the same status from the same source refreshes. Enemy `statusDurationScale` (0.5 for bosses) shortens only `control` statuses; DoTs are never shortened. Tags show stack counts ("PSN x3", "CHILL x3"). Tests: `dots.test.mjs`, `control.test.mjs`, `stat-debuffs.test.mjs`. Gambler's Dice crit debuffs: Chill, Poison, Stun or Shock. Hero statuses are cleared when a floor is cleared. STATUS ICONS (user's choice, Risk of Rain style): every status has its own 5x5 pixel icon + outline (7x7, `src/data/statusIcons.json`, `_order` = display order: control, DoT, debuffs, buffs; made by `docs/proposals/make-status-icons.mjs`), drawn at CHAR_SCALE in ROWS OF 3 over the health bar (rows go up; a hero's item dots move up above them), stack count in small white digits just right of the icon, the icon hops a pixel when it arrives or gains a stack and blinks in its last 0.8s (`BattleScene.drawChips()/blinkIcons()`, layout in `src/systems/statusIcons.js`, texture `statusIconTexture.js`; a status without an icon falls back to its old text tag). No more text tags, the shield amount shows only as the blue bar. Tests: `status-icons.test.mjs`. STATUS EFFECTS ON THE BODY (user's choice: EVERY status plays its own loop at the same time, the full version from the effects page): `src/systems/statusFx.js` (no Phaser; one recipe per status in `LOOPS`, pixels in character pixels around the feet; `statusLook(kinds, bodyBox, t)` returns draws + one body tint (first match in `TINTS`) + `ice` + a small shake `dx`): stun stars round the head, freeze = the sprite turns to ice from the feet up (`Character.setStatusLook()`, `iceColor()`; textures cached per tint/ice step) with crystals and floor frost, the ice breaks off in shards when it ends (`iceBreak`), chill snow + paler per stack, shock crackles + twitch, fear shivers + sweat drop + tremble, taunt red "!" beside the head, silence padlock beside the head, weaken dull + dust, armor break chips + cracks, curse orbiting skull, blind smoke band over the eyes, mark corner brackets, bleed drops (more stacks = more drops), burn flames + embers, poison bubbles, buff gold sparkles, regen plus signs, shield bubble, invulnerable gold rims, parry petals. Drawn every frame by `BattleScene.drawStatusFx()/paintFx()` on one Graphics (a broken recipe is caught and skipped, never stops the fight). Tests: `status-fx.test.mjs`.
- **Shields and Invulnerable (D1):** statuses `shield` and `invulnerable` (statuses.json). ALL health loss from hits, damage over time and thorns goes through `BattleState.dealDamage()`. SHIELD: soaks damage before health, oldest shield first; size = `amount` + `percentMaxHp` of the holder + `attackMultiplier` x the caster's attack; lasts `durationMs`, or until broken if none is given; the same source+skill refills instead of stacking, different sources stack. A shield used up by damage is BROKEN (`shieldBreak` event, optional `onBreak: { damageMultiplier, damageType }` = shards hit every enemy of the holder, damage from the caster's attack); one that just runs out of time EXPIRES (a normal status expire event, no explosion). A hit fully soaked by a shield skips lifesteal, thorns and crit debuffs. INVULNERABLE: no damage at all (hits, DoTs, thorns), `healPercentOnEnd` heals a % of max health when it ends (Divine Shield). Drawn as a thin light-blue bar over the health bar, a "SHIELD 75" tag, "Shield -4" and "Immune!" popups, "Shield broken!" + a small shake. No hero uses them yet (heroes come in phase E). Tests: `shields.test.mjs`.
- **Taunt (D2):** a taunted enemy MUST attack its taunter (`BattleState.tauntedBy()` / `pickTarget()`): melee enemies walk to the taunter, ranged ones shoot it. Taunt beats an enemy's opponent and tap focus rules; when it ends the enemy goes back to its old opponent. The NEWEST living taunter wins (an older taunt takes over again if the newer taunter dies); the same source refreshes. When a taunt lands while an attack is on the way to someone else, a melee swing is dropped (`attackCancel` 'taunted', it walks to the taunter) and an arrow bends to the taunter (`attackRetarget`). Bosses take taunt for half as long (`statusDurationScale`). A proc's status spec can pick its own receiver with `to` (`self` / `squad` / `target`), e.g. the Knight's planned Taunt Wall: one proc with `{ status: 'taunt', to: 'target' }` AND `{ status: 'buff', mods: [defense], to: 'self' }` (taunts the enemy it hit, the Knight alone gets the armor). A thin red line from each taunted enemy to its taunter shows who it is forced to hit. No hero has the passive yet (Knight comes in E1). Tests: `taunt.test.mjs`.
- Status body effects (shield bubble, etc.) are not drawn while an enemy is still sliding in with its floor (`v.ch.parentContainer`), like the elite glow: they used to show at its final spot before it arrived (user, 2026-10-19).
