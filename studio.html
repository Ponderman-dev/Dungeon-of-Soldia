Drawing board

Pick a part, then draw.

Each square is one pixel. Drawing outside the part makes it bigger; erasing its edges shrinks it. The anchor (crosshair) is the point the part turns around: put a sword's anchor on its handle. Keys: B pencil, E eraser, L line, R rectangle, G fill, I pick colour, A anchor, M move, S mirror drawing (the dashed blue middle line). On iPad: two-finger tap = undo, three-finger tap = redo, touch and hold with a finger = pick that colour.

Parts

Parts drawn later sit in front. A part named weapon is the one that swings and glows; the side it is on is the way the character looks.
