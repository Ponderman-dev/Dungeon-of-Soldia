# Skills

Hero skills, wind-up, area skills.
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **Skills:** each hero has ONE attack skill and ONE support skill (`skills: { attack, support }` in heroes.json; every skill in skills.json has `kind` attack/support; `validateHeroSkills()` checks it). Slot 0 = attack (left square, red), slot 1 = support (right square, green); colours in `SKILL_COLORS` (config.js). The Archer's support is a PLACEHOLDER (Eagle Focus) until his redo (Hunter's Mark). Tap a skill square under the hero to cast. One tap: single-target skills hit the focused enemy, else the hero's usual target. Each skill has a cooldown (cooldowns only run during fights). No mana cost. WIND-UP (C1): a tapped skill goes on cooldown at once and winds up for `castMs` (per skill in skills.json, else combat.json `skillWindUp.defaultCastMs` 700, longer than a swing): `castStart` event, a charge bar in the skill colour fills under the hero's health bar; then it goes off (`cast`, targets picked at that moment). While winding up the hero does not attack, walk or start another skill. Stunned, frozen or silenced during the wind-up = the skill is LOST and STAYS on cooldown (user's choice; `castCancel`, 'Interrupted!'). Tests: `skill-windup.test.mjs`. SKILL LOOK (C2, combat.json `skillFx`): when a skill goes off the caster flashes in the skill colour (attack orange, support green), a ring bursts on each target (attack) or around the caster (support), the skill NAME floats over the hero once (user's pick B, 2026-10-18: CAPITALS, fixed 16px, never scaled, slides up + fades; colour per hero in combat.json `skillFx.name.heroColors`; `BattleScene.skillName()`; no name at the wind-up any more), skill damage numbers are bigger, orange and pop in; attack skills shake the screen and freeze the fight for a split second (hit-stop). `BattleScene.skillBurst()`. SKILL ANIMATION: heroes use their own pose for skills, not the normal swing (`Character.castWindUp/castRelease/castCancel`): during the wind-up the hero crouches a little and cocks its weapon back; an attack skill then does a big weapon slash with a hop, a support skill a high jump with the weapon lifted; an interrupted wind-up returns to the normal pose. `castLift` is added to the hero's height in `followPositions()`. Skill damage = attack x `damageMultiplier`, uses the skill's `damageType` or the hero's. Targets: `enemy`, `allEnemies`, `self`. Skills can `apply` statuses.
- **AREA skills (user: 'proper AoE, by position'):** a skill with `target: allEnemies` and an `area` in skills.json only hits the enemies standing inside it (its target always is): `circle` (`at` 'target' or 'caster', `radius` px, `ySquash` 0.7 = flatter oval), `cone` from the caster towards its target (`range`, `angleDeg`), `band` = a strip across the field at the target's height (`halfHeight`). Ground Pound circle 105 around his target, Frost Nova circle 120, Rain of Arrows circle 110 (a channel: the zone FOLLOWS its main target while it lives), Crescendo circle 125, Fan of Knives cone 70° / 560px, Wind Cutter band ±55px. `BattleState.areaHit()/inZone()`, the `cast` and `wave` events carry `zone`; the scene draws it on the floor (`showZone()`, combat.json `skillFx.areaColor/areaMs`). Without positions or without `area`: every living enemy (old tests). Tests: `area-skills.test.mjs`.
- **SKILL ICONS (user, 2026-10-18; review page https://claude.ai/artifact/66p6zRjp6S78Gg9rAk6YSs):** every hero skill and passive has a 16x16 pixel icon in `src/data/skillIcons.json` (key = skill id, `passive_<heroId>`), drawn by `scripts/art/skill-icons.mjs` (one pen recipe per icon; edit and re-run). Textures: `src/entities/skillIconTexture.js` (`skillIconKey(scene, id, framed)`, `bookPageIconKey(scene, page, golden)`). FIGHT: the skill squares show the icon at x2 instead of the 2 letters (a skill without an icon still shows `short`); the square's line is red / green when ready, grey while cooling (the icon darkens and the seconds show on it); a ROTATION skill (Mage) always shows the NEXT spell's element colour as its line (user). The PASSIVE icon (gold frame) sits left of the squares (buttons moved 10px right); tap = a small window with its name and text for 3.5s (`showPassive`); ANY tap closes it at once (that tap still works normally), tapping the icon again closes it too (user, 2026-10-20). Hero card (squad screen): the P / A / S badges are the icons. Skill BOOK pages: the book picture is recoloured by page type (skillBooks.json `pageColors`: stat blue, Debuff purple, Heal green, Defense steel, Team teal, Tempo yellow, Risk red, Area orange, Self pink; golden book stays gold).
- **STAY AT IMPACT (user, 2026-10-20):** Defense Bash, Multistrike and Savage Leap (skills.json `staysAtImpact`) really move the hero in the RULES when the skill fires (`BattleState.moveToImpact()`: a dash goes the distance minus `stopPx` between `minPx`/`maxPx` of combat.json `skillFx.dash.<id>`, a leap `share` of the way from `skillFx.look.<id>.leap`; never past the target's push-apart edge, so it is not shoved back out). The cast event carries `moved` = where it came from; `dashAt()` / `leapAt()` slide the hero over from there and do NOT go back. Test: `stay-at-impact.test.mjs`.
