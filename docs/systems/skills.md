# Skills

Hero skills, wind-up, area skills.
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **Skills:** each hero has ONE attack skill and ONE support skill (`skills: { attack, support }` in heroes.json; every skill in skills.json has `kind` attack/support; `validateHeroSkills()` checks it). Slot 0 = attack (left square, red), slot 1 = support (right square, green); colours in `SKILL_COLORS` (config.js). The Archer's support is a PLACEHOLDER (Eagle Focus) until his redo (Hunter's Mark). Tap a skill square under the hero to cast. One tap: single-target skills hit the focused enemy, else the hero's usual target. Each skill has a cooldown (cooldowns only run during fights). No mana cost. WIND-UP (C1): a tapped skill goes on cooldown at once and winds up for `castMs` (per skill in skills.json, else combat.json `skillWindUp.defaultCastMs` 700, longer than a swing): `castStart` event, the skill name pops up and a charge bar in the skill colour fills under the hero's health bar; then it goes off (`cast`, targets picked at that moment). While winding up the hero does not attack, walk or start another skill. Stunned, frozen or silenced during the wind-up = the skill is LOST and STAYS on cooldown (user's choice; `castCancel`, 'Interrupted!'). Tests: `skill-windup.test.mjs`. SKILL LOOK (C2, combat.json `skillFx`): when a skill goes off the caster flashes in the skill colour (attack orange, support green), a ring bursts on each target (attack) or around the caster (support), the skill name shows as a big banner, skill damage numbers are bigger, orange and pop in; attack skills shake the screen and freeze the fight for a split second (hit-stop). `BattleScene.skillBurst()`. SKILL ANIMATION: heroes use their own pose for skills, not the normal swing (`Character.castWindUp/castRelease/castCancel`): during the wind-up the hero crouches a little and cocks its weapon back; an attack skill then does a big weapon slash with a hop, a support skill a high jump with the weapon lifted; an interrupted wind-up returns to the normal pose. `castLift` is added to the hero's height in `followPositions()`. Skill damage = attack x `damageMultiplier`, uses the skill's `damageType` or the hero's. Targets: `enemy`, `allEnemies`, `self`. Skills can `apply` statuses.
- **AREA skills (user: 'proper AoE, by position'):** a skill with `target: allEnemies` and an `area` in skills.json only hits the enemies standing inside it (its target always is): `circle` (`at` 'target' or 'caster', `radius` px, `ySquash` 0.7 = flatter oval), `cone` from the caster towards its target (`range`, `angleDeg`), `band` = a strip across the field at the target's height (`halfHeight`). Ground Pound circle 105 around his target, Frost Nova circle 120, Rain of Arrows circle 110 (a channel: the zone FOLLOWS its main target while it lives), Crescendo circle 125, Fan of Knives cone 70° / 560px, Wind Cutter band ±55px. `BattleState.areaHit()/inZone()`, the `cast` and `wave` events carry `zone`; the scene draws it on the floor (`showZone()`, combat.json `skillFx.areaColor/areaMs`). Without positions or without `area`: every living enemy (old tests). Tests: `area-skills.test.mjs`.
