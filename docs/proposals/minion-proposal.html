Soldia minion sprites

The 8 minions are still coloured blocks. Here is a simple pixel sprite for each one, in the same style as the heroes and enemies: 3 to 5 colours, no tiny details. Each card shows the old block and the new sprite at game size, next to the hero that brings it, plus a big view of the new one.

Smaller than heroesWeak minions (Skeleton, Rat, Wisp, Imp, Mimic) are clearly smaller than a hero so you can tell them apart in a crowd. Greater minions (Gargoyle, Spirit Knight, Phoenix) are close to hero size.
Built from partsLike the heroes, each sprite is split into parts that can move: the Skeleton's and Imp's weapons swing, the Mimic's lid chomps, the Phoenix's wing flaps.
Easy to editOnce you like them they go into the game and show up in the Character Studio, where you can redraw any pixel.
