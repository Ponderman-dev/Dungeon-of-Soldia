Projectiles: what flies

Every shot in the game that flies from a fighter to its target, drawn by the game's own code at the game's speed and size. Heroes stand at the bottom, enemies at the top, like in a fight. Skill shots are not here: they are part of the hero skill looks you already approved. For each shot, tap OK or CHANGE and write what you want (bigger, a different colour, a trail, a spin, a different hit when it lands...). Tap a scene to replay it. Slow x4 lets you see each pixel.
