# Bosses

Every 20 floors.
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **BOSSES (every 20 floors, user's choice):** dungeons.json `A.bosses` = a fixed line-up per floor (`enemiesForFloor()` checks it first): 20 Grukk the War Drum (+ War Drum object: goblins near it attack 30% faster, it beats up goblins; Cleaver Charge on the BACK hero + knockback, Rally Shout heals his side at 50%), 40 Brood Mother (lays Egg Sacs that hatch into 3 spiderlings after 6s, Web Spit = webbed = stuck like a stun for 3s), 60 Frost Warden (3 armor PLATES = 70% less damage until broken, fire breaks them 3x faster; immune to chill/freeze; Blizzard channel, Ice Slam), 80 Rask & Vesh (linked twins: one falls, the other ENRAGES and raises it after 8s at 30% unless it falls too; Switch! swaps their spots; Smoke & Daggers), 100 Morthul (at 0 health he turns to DUST and a PHYLACTERY crystal appears: break it in 10s or he reforms at 50%, each crystal 1.5x tougher; Raise Dead, Soul Drain channel, Curse Wave), 120 Ignatius (HEAT gauge fills over time and from fire hits, ice/chill/freeze cool him; full = ERUPTION on the squad), 140 Sela (glass clones of every hero at the start and at 50%; Mirror Ward bounces the next hero skill back; Shatter = clones burst; Glass Prison silences), 160 Vharox (at 75/50/25% flies into the SKY = cannot be hit, lightning every 1s and 3 Storm Sprites: kill them all to drag him down stunned and +25% damage taken). All data in enemies.json (`boss`, `object`, `abilities` with `castMs` wind-ups, `aura`, `hatch`, `plates`, `statusImmune`, `link`, `phylactery`, `heat`, `mirror`, `sky`; format at the top of `src/systems/bosses.js`, whose `bossMethods` are mixed into BattleState). A boss wind-up shows a RED bar under its health bar (a channel = purple, emptying); a stun/freeze/fear/knockback INTERRUPTS it. Boss bar at the top of the screen (`drawBossHud()`: name, big health bar, heat gauge, plate pips). Scene events in `BattleScene.showBossEvent()` (enemySpawn, revive, hatch, dust, reform, bossAbility = red banner + rings, bossChannel, drain, plateBreak, immuneStatus, enrage, swap, skyPhase/grounded, reflect). `def.drawScale` = pixel size (default CHAR_SCALE; spiderling 2). Sprites from the proposal (`scripts/art/boss-sprites.mjs`, written once by `scripts/art/add-bosses.mjs`). The balance bot taps objects first like a player. Boss toughness (phase H, user: 'make tougher bosses'): Grukk and Rask & Vesh unchanged; Brood Mother 2500 health / 12 attack, Frost Warden 3600/13, Morthul 3600/12, Ignatius 3800/13, Sela 3500/12, Vharox 4600/14, so each boss beats about 1 in 4 of the squads that reach it in the review bot (Grukk ~18%). `node scripts/tests/bosscheck.mjs [runsPerSquad] [floors]` = every squad plays up to a boss floor protected from dying, then fights the boss for real (win %, fight length, health left). Tests: `bosses.test.mjs`.
- **Boss stun/slow:** bosses take half-length stun and slow (value lives in the boss JSON).
