# Devlog: A title screen, a camp to rest in, and heroes who talk (v0.1.0)

Hello adventurers!

Dungeon of Soldia is a pocket-sized auto-battler roguelike: pick a squad of 3 heroes, send them down into the
dungeon and see how deep they get. Heroes fight on their own; you tap enemies to focus them, drag a hero onto an
enemy to give an order, and fire each hero's skills when they are ready. Fallen heroes stay fallen, so every floor
counts.

This update is all about **arriving** in the dungeon.

## New: the title screen
The game now opens on a moonlit forest seen from above, stretching to the horizon. Far away, a lone tower pokes out
of the trees: that is where the dungeon begins. Stars twinkle, clouds drift past the full moon. Tap to start and the
camera flies to the tower.

## New: the camp
Before every descent you now arrive at a small camp by a campfire, and one of your unlocked heroes walks in to warm
up by the fire. Around the fire you will meet four new faces:

- **The Old Scholar** (Glossary)
- **The Captain** (Heroes)
- **The Herald** (Leaderboard)
- **The Merchant** (Shop)

They are still setting up shop, so for now they will only tell you "Coming soon". The same goes for your Profile and
the Settings gear.

When you are ready, tap **TO DUNGEON**: your hero heads off past the fire and you choose your squad for the run.

## Also new: heroes speak
Your heroes now say a line at the start of a run and after every cleared floor, with special lines when a boss
appears, when a friend falls, or when they barely survive.

## Everything else since the last build

### Combat
- Tapping a skill now cuts the hero's normal attack short: the skill animation starts right away.
- Melee heroes now step in to their target when they hit.
- Defense Bash, Multistrike and Savage Leap: the hero now stays where they land instead of hopping back.
- The **Mimic** is now bait: it never attacks, it draws the enemies around it to itself, and it still explodes.
- The Flame Spirit is now the **Dark Spirit** (its orbs always dealt dark damage).
- Minions: element minions get half of their hero's element bonus, the Ice Spirit Knight hits with ice, the
  Phoenix burns and slowly heals itself, and the Gargoyle is tougher.

### New items
- **Thunderstruck** (legendary): sometimes a circle grows under the strongest enemy, then a giant thunderbolt hits it.
- **Frozen Thunderbolt** (legendary): electric hits also chill; at 3 Chill the enemy shatters and hurts its neighbours.
- **Eclipse Idol** (legendary): stores your dark damage, then Dooms 2-3 enemies (slower, and heavy dark damage over time).
- **Guardian's Banner** (epic): the whole squad gets more defense and resist while its holder lives.
- **Assault Breastplate** (epic): lots of defense, and every enemy has less defense while its holder lives.

### Changed items
- Element charms add a small extra fire / ice / electric / dark hit to every attack. Extra copies merge into one
  bigger hit (one damage number instead of many).
- Chill Band, Magma Core and Ember Charm hit or burn more often. Ember Charm, Venom Fang and Thousand Cuts: extra
  copies give a better chance and a stronger effect.
- **Spirit Axe**: when its holder is hit, a ghost axe spins once around them and hits every nearby enemy hard.
- **Sucker Punch Glove**: any hit on an enemy above 90% health deals extra damage.
- **Bandage Roll** now cures a burn / poison / bleed and heals a little.
- **Martyr's Shield** is now a bodyguard: its holder takes the hits aimed at a low-health ally for a few seconds.
- **Spiked Aegis**: while shielded, melee attackers take part of their hit back.
- **Catalyst Vial**: burn, poison and bleed ticks can now crit.
- Removed: **Fire Arrows** (its burn never worked) and **Last Stand Banner** (other items already do its job).

### Balance
- Enemies get tougher a little faster as you go deeper.
- Epic and legendary items drop less often, so each one feels special.

### Look
- "Who gets it?" (choosing which hero gets an item) has tidier hero rows: name and level on one line, then what
  changes, then the hero's items.
- After a floor, the hero's speech bubble now stays up a little longer (1.2 s) before the reward screen.
- New pixel font for the whole interface.
- The sound on/off button is now a little speaker icon.
- "Floor cleared!" now shows in the middle of the screen.

### Sound
- New menu sounds: button taps, menu windows opening (a page turn) and closing, scrolling the hero list, and a deep
  gong when you tap BEGIN DESCENT.
- The title screen and the camp now have sound too (taps, footsteps).

### Fixes
- Fixed a freeze when a Kobold Sapper's bomb landed after the Sapper was already gone.
- Kobold Sapper bombs: heroes no longer get stuck at the edge of the bomb circle; whoever stands in it gets hit.

## Good to know
- Your progress (best floor, unlocked heroes) is saved in your browser.
- Sound effects are in; music is not yet.
- Best played on a phone in portrait mode (or use the fullscreen button).
