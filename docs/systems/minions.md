# Minions

Weak and greater minions (D4).
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **Minions (D4, `minions.json`):** extra fighters on the hero side (`BattleState.minions`, NEVER in `this.heroes`: no XP, perks or morale, never count as living heroes, don't feed counters). Made by SOURCES (`hero.minionSources`, `buildSources()`): the hero's own passive `def.spawner` (Necromancer's Skeleton Summoner: weak, no upkeep) and minion ITEMS the hero holds (items.js effects `minionSpawner` = weak, `greaterMinion: 'id'` = greater; only the holder gets them). WEAK (`{ minion, minMs, maxMs, cap, atFloorStart }`): `startFloor()` (scene calls it when a fight begins) raises the floor-start ones (EVERY weak source raises one at the start of each fight, `atFloorStart` default 1, user's choice: no waiting the first 10-15s), `tickSpawner()` raises one every minMs..maxMs while enemies live, over the `cap` the OLDEST from that source is pushed out (`minionVanish` 'pushedOut'); each copy of a weak spawner item is its own source; they vanish when the floor is won (`endFloor()` from `onWin`) or their holder dies (`removeMinionsOf()`). GREATER (`kind: 'greater'` in minions.json): ONE per item type, extra copies only make it stronger (`minions.copyStatBonus` 0.5 of its health and attack per extra copy, also if a copy is picked up later); summoned at the start of a fight if there is none; stays between floors with its health (no win heal; only healing/buffs help it); it walks home after a win and through the door with the squad (`livingHeroViews()` includes greater minions, `slotX/slotY`); when it dies it comes back after `minions.revivalFloors` (2) cleared floors, at the start of the floor after that (killed on floor N: floors N and N+1 cleared, back at the start of N+2; `sourceState.wait`, `endFloor()` counts down); it vanishes if its holder dies. UPKEEP (combat.json `minions`): for every minion ITEM of a hero that has a live minion the holder loses 12% attack speed and 12% defense (`upkeepAttackSpeedPercent`/`upkeepDefensePercent`, percent of the level stat, `refreshStats()`), the hero passive costs nothing, upkeep ends when the minions are gone. Minions fight like the other fighters (move, melee/ranged AI, nearest foe; they ignore tap focus), enemies can attack them (`defenders()` = living heroes + minions, nearest wins; taunt works; RANGED minions are skipped while anyone else stands: `frontDefenders()`, see below), minion stats are fixed from minions.json (`refreshMinionStats()`; squad-wide buffs reach them via `squadUnits()`, buffs never change their health), statuses and shields work on them. A dying minion: `death` event only (no XP/morale). Scene: `showMinion()` (dark puff + the figure grows out of the floor), `puff()`. No hero or item uses them yet (Necromancer in E8, items in G). Tests: `minions.test.mjs`, `greater-minions.test.mjs`.
- **Element minions (session 17):** `holderElementShare` (minions.json, 0.5 on Imp, Wisp, Phoenix, Ice Spirit Knight) = the minion's `damageBonus` for its element is that share of its holder's (`refreshMinionStats()`). `hitType` = the element of its hits when it moves as melee (Ice Spirit Knight; same field elites use). `regenPercent` = own regen per second (Phoenix; `update()`). Phoenix `onAttackApply` burn; the old `onHitHeal` (squad heal when hit) is gone. Gargoyle defense 14.
- **Mimic = bait (session 17):** `def.bait` { radius, durationMs }: it never attacks; each attack turn (once in reach) `baitTaunt()` taunts every enemy within radius, then it dies and explodes. Health 14, defense 0.

- **ENEMIES SKIP RANGED MINIONS (user, 2026-10-21: 'enemies attack a ranged summon at the back rather than the hero in front'):** `pickTarget()` (enemy side) and the boss single-target picks (burrow, daggers, glass clones, sky strike in bosses.js) use `frontDefenders()` = defenders minus ranged minions (Wisp, Imp, Phoenix); only when nobody else is left do they become targets. Taunt and area hits still reach them. Test in `minions.test.mjs`. Curve 3 smart (same dice): mean 56.9 -> 49.4, median 42 -> 40 (the ranged minions had been soaking hits for the heroes).
