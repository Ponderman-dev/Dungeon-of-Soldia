swaps their spots; Smoke & Daggers), 100 Morthul (at 0 health he turns to DUST and a PHYLACTERY crystal appears: break it in 10s or he reforms at 50%, each crystal 1.5x tougher; at most `maxReforms` (2) comebacks, then the next crystal running out ends him for good (user, 2026-10-19: ~10% of Morthul fights never ended); Raise Dead, Soul Drain channel, Curse Wave), 120 Ignatius (HEAT gauge fills over time and from fire hits, ice/chill/freeze cool him; full = ERUPTION on the squad), 140 Sela (glass clones of every hero at the start and at 50%; Mirror Ward bounces the next hero skill back; Shatter = clones burst; Glass Prison silences), 160 Vharox (at 75/50/25% flies into the SKY = cannot be hit, lightning every 1s and 3 Storm Sprites: kill them all to drag him down stunned and +25% damage taken). All data in enemies.json (`boss`, `object`, `abilities` with `castMs` wind-ups, `aura`, `hatch`, `plates`, `statusImmune`, `link`, `phylactery`, `heat`, `mirror`, `sky`; format at the top of `src/systems/bosses.js`, whose `bossMethods` are mixed into BattleState). A boss wind-up shows a RED bar under its health bar (a channel = purple, emptying); a stun/freeze/fear/knockback INTERRUPTS it. Boss bar at the top of the screen (`drawBossHud()`: name, big health bar, heat gauge, plate pips). Scene events in `BattleScene.showBossEvent()` (enemySpawn, revive, hatch, dust, reform, bossAbility = red banner + rings, bossChannel, drain, plateBreak, immuneStatus, enrage, swap, skyPhase/grounded, reflect). `def.drawScale` = pixel size (default CHAR_SCALE; spiderling 2). Sprites from the proposal (`scripts/art/boss-sprites.mjs`, written once by `scripts/art/add-bosses.mjs`). The balance bot taps objects first like a player. Boss toughness (phase H, user: 'make tougher bosses'): Grukk and Rask & Vesh unchanged; Brood Mother 2500 health / 6 attack (was 12), Frost Warden 3600/10 (was 13), Grukk attack 7 (was 8): lowered 2026-10-19 when bosses started swinging from their own edge (movement.md), to keep the same win rates, Morthul 3600/12, Ignatius 3800/13, Sela 3500/12, Vharox 4600/14, so each boss beats about 1 in 4 of the squads that reach it in the review bot (Grukk ~18%). `node scripts/tests/bosscheck.mjs [runsPerSquad] [floors]` = every squad plays up to a boss floor protected from dying, then fights the boss for real (win %, fight length, health left; heroes that still fall on the protected floors are revived before the next boss: before 2026-10-19 they were not, so bosses 80+ 'won' in under 1s against already-dead squads and the old numbers were wrong). Fixed-tool numbers after the reach fix + retune (2 runs/squad): Grukk 93%, Brood Mother 73%, Frost Warden 55%, Rask ~10%, Morthul ~67% (endless crystal loops gone: maxReforms), Ignatius ~32%, Sela ~85%, Vharox ~43%. Tests: `bosses.test.mjs`.
- **Boss stun/slow:** bosses take half-length stun and slow (value lives in the boss JSON).
- **Boss start (screen review, user):** when the squad is in place on a boss floor, `BattleScene.bossRoar()` runs: the boss ROARS (cue `bossRoar`), a very hard camera shake, a white flash, manga ACTION LINES (white wedges from a ring round the boss out past the screen edge, redrawn at random every 60ms) and a huge "ROAR!!" that slams in and wobbles. The fight (with the old `bossIntro` gong) starts 2s after the roar began. Numbers: combat.json `transition.bossIntro`.
