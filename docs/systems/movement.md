# Movement

Where fighters stand and how they walk (rules side, not the scene).
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **MOVEMENT (rebuilt, Warcraft-3 style, user's drawing):** positions live in the RULES, not the scene. Every fighter has `x, y` (its feet, screen pixels) once `BattleState.place()` was called (the scene does it in `startFighting()`; tests/bot use `placeLines()` with `movement.layout`). Every update (`BattleState.move()`): a MELEE fighter walks straight at its target at `movement.walkSpeedPxPerSec` (170) until the target is inside its reach oval (`reachX` 64 sideways, `reachY` 94 up/down), then swings; both sides walk so two melee fighters meet in the middle, face to face. RANGED fighters stay where they are and shoot. Fighters closer than the `separateX/Y` oval push apart (`separatePushPerSec`; ranged ones hold their ground), so a crowd spreads around a target instead of stacking. Pushing is GENTLE (user: "a little push is fine", crowds used to slide into the walls): a fighter standing still gives way only `settledPushShare` (0.3) as much as a walking one, two standing fighters drift apart at `settledPairPush` (0.4) of the speed, and within `wallSoftPx` (60) of a side wall a fighter is hardly pushed further towards it (test `crowd.test.mjs`). Feared = walks back to its own spot; knocked back = pushed `knockbackPx` towards its own side over `knockbackMs`; stunned/frozen or mid-swing = stands still. Nobody can move faster than walking + pushing, so no teleporting or sliding. The scene only DRAWS: `followPositions()` puts each figure at its position every frame, with a hop per step while `moving` (`attackMotion.hopHeight` / `hopLengthPx`, rhythm from `walked`); lower heroes are drawn in front. FACING: every fighter is mirrored left/right to look at whoever it fights (its swing target, its opponent, or the last thing it shot at; `Character.face()`, the art looks the way its weapon part points); heroes look the normal way again after a win. HEALTH BARS are always drawn on top of every character: enemy bars/tags slide in with their floor, then leave the floor container in `startFighting()` and get the `heroBar` depth. Targets: enemies keep their opponent, a new one is the NEAREST hero by real distance (`nearest()`); melee heroes likewise; ranged heroes pick at random; tap-focus wins. After a win heroes walk back to their slots at walking speed. Without positions (some rule tests) nobody moves and melee can always hit. Tests: `movement.test.mjs` (walk, meet, speed, ranged stay, no teleport, no stacking, retarget, fear, knockback, targets).
