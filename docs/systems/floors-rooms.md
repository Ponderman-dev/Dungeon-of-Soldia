# Floor flow and special rooms

Door walk between floors, special rooms, dungeons.
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **Floor flow:** each floor has a door in the back wall (top of screen): `src/entities/Door.js` (user: nothing fancy: a plain purple door in a purple arch, CLOSED during the fight; `open()` swings its two halves open and the doorway lights up only a little, a soft lavender glow; also used on the squad screen). Heroes walking into the door FADE OUT over the last `transition.doorFadePx` (70) px (`walkPath` option `fadeLastPx`) instead of being covered. After a clear: door opens, living heroes heal, they gather into a single-file line at the centre of the map and walk up it into the door (steady pace, hopping each step, smaller with distance, NO fading: the door's inside is drawn in front of them), the camera slides up (old floor drops away, new floor with its enemies comes down from the top), then the living heroes come up from the bottom centre in a line and fan out to their slots, and the fight starts. A dead hero freezes (idle animation stops) and stays dimmed in their slot during the fight; when the survivors go through the door the dead hero's figure disappears (the slot box stays dim, no skill squares). Skill squares are hidden together with the slot boxes during transitions. Health bars are hidden while walking. The hero slot boxes and name labels are hidden for the whole transition (also at game start) and fade back in when the fight starts. Timings and speeds are in combat.json `transition`.
- **SPECIAL ROOMS (user's choice):** from floor 4 about 1 floor in 4 (`rooms.json` `fromFloor` / `chance`) is a ROOM instead of a fight: never a boss floor, never the floor before a boss, never two in a row; the floor number still goes up (`roomForFloor()` in `src/systems/rooms.js`). Kinds by `weight`: TREASURE (1 chest, 40% two; each tapped chest opens its lid and gives a normal 1-of-3 item pick), MIMIC (its own room, but it shows the SAME name and hint as a treasure room, "Treasure Room" / "Tap a chest to open it" (screen review: a surprise): one chest that shakes and becomes the `mimic` enemy in enemies.json (900 health / 16 attack base, tougher than an Orc Brute, scaled by floor like any enemy); beating it gives a pick with `rewardOdds` rare 70 / epic 30 instead of the normal reward), REST FOUNTAIN (heals every living hero 50% + `BattleState.bless()`: the next 3 FIGHTS start with a `regenBoost` of 0.5% max health/s, set in `startFloor()`), SKILL DEALER (a book of 5 pages: skillBooks.json `cards.dealer` / `contents.dealer` 2 stat + 3 modifier; RewardScene shows >3 pages as COMPACT cards, `pageLook()`), PUZZLE (`PuzzleScene`: one of the 5 puzzles the user KEPT from the proposal page (session 6; the 1-8 slide puzzle is gone), picked at random: MIMIC SHUFFLE (coin into a chest, chests swap, tap the right one; a wrong chest is the `mimic` minion sprite and bites one hero `mimicBiteHealthPercent` 10% of max health via `BattleState.trapDamage()`, never kills), RUNE LOCK (repeat a growing order of runes, `runeGlyphs` 5x5 in rooms.json; a wrong rune = nothing), MIRROR BEAM (tap mirrors to bounce light into the crystal; `makeBeamLevel()` tries every mirror setting so a room is ALWAYS solvable and starts unsolved), ICE SLIDE (the key slides until rock/wall, stop it on the keyhole; `makeIceLevel()` searches the stops so it is ALWAYS solvable, `par` = fewest moves; arrow buttons + swipes), PICTURE PUZZLE (a dungeon picture drawn from the game's pixel data, cut into pieces, tap two to swap; shallow floors have a PEEK button). Harder deeper: rooms.json `kinds.puzzle.tiers` per puzzle (fromFloor 0 / 25 / 50). Rules: `src/systems/puzzles.js`, tests `puzzles.test.mjs`. Solved = an item pick, failed / 'Give up' = nothing. Dev: `?room=puzzle&puzzle=<kind>`). Props (chest, fountain, dealer, tablet) are pixel characters in `src/data/roomProps.json` (chest drawScale 4, tablet 8: big, screen review), kept out of the squad's walk to the door (user): an emptied chest and the used tablet sink into the floor in a puff (`vanishProp()`), the dealer walks to the side wall when you are done (`stepAside()`), the fountain stands at the side (`rooms.json` fountain `x` 92); a gold arrow bobs over each tappable prop, the room name + a hint show in the middle. Scene side: `src/scenes/roomMethods.js` mixed into BattleScene (`beginFloor()` = fight or `startRoom()`, `tapProp()`, `leaveRoom()` opens the door, `goToNextFloor()` = the door walk, also used after a won fight); `rollItems(odds)`, `rollChoices({ odds })`. Dev server only: `?room=<kind>` makes every odd floor that room and `window.__game` is set (browser checks). The balance bots (review.mjs etc.) still play every floor as a fight. Tests: `rooms.test.mjs`.
- **Dungeons:** floors, enemies and bosses are grouped by dungeon id ("A") so Dungeon B is data-only.
- **FLOOR ART (screen review, user: 'more natural looking floor, dirt and grass, an old dungeon, pillars on the wall'):** every floor (fights, rooms, and the squad screen) is one pixel-art image drawn by `src/systems/floorArt.js` (no Phaser; seeded, so a floor always looks the same) and made into a texture by `src/entities/floorTexture.js` (2 screen px per art px). Wall: stone bricks, two round PILLARS, moss at the foot, hanging vines. Floor: worn flagstones (some sunken), dirt in the gaps, cracks, dirt patches with broken stone chunks, grass tufts, moss, pebbles, the wall's shadow, darker side edges. All numbers and colours in `src/data/floorArt.json`; each tile colour (dungeons.json `tileColors`) has `variants` layouts.
- **DOOR LOOK (screen review, user: 'the door looks off with the floor'):** `src/entities/Door.js` is now a stone arch of blocks (keystone) with a dark wooden plank door, two iron bands with rivets and ring handles; open = the halves swing to the sides onto a BLACK doorway (no more lavender glow). Grass/moss/vines are a dull dry brown-olive (user: desaturated, then browner) to blend with the stone. Colours: floorArt.json `door`, `floor.grass`, `floor.moss`. DOOR OPENING (user): `Door.open()` = an unlock rattle, the heavy halves swing open faster and faster and darken as they turn (a shadow layer per half), bounce off the wall, then `slam()`: a thud shake, dust puffs at the foot and dust falling from the arch (timings: floorArt.json `door.anim`). BIG ENTRANCE (user: 'very big door, like an entrance to a dungeon, very intimidating'): the squad screen uses `new Door(..., { big: true })` (sizes floorArt.json `door.big`): a much bigger arch and door (3 iron bands, claw marks), a skull keystone, portcullis spikes under the arch, chains beside it, bones on the floor, a heavier slam with more dust, and red eyes that blink in the dark doorway after it opens. Bigger wall torches with a glow beside it; the BEGIN DESCENT button moved to the bottom over RANDOM (`BEGIN_Y`; layout in saving-squad.md).
