# Core fight rules

Damage maths, attack timing, perks, morale, levelling, tap focus and drag orders.
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

All numbers below go in JSON/config so they are easy to tune.
- **Damage types:** physical (melee, ranged) and magical (fire, ice, electric, dark). Every attack and skill has one type.
- **Defense:** % cut of PHYSICAL damage. No hard cap: a curve (`armorPercent()` in combat.js, `armorCurve.linearUpTo` 50 in combat.json): up to 50 points 1 point = 1% (10 defense = 10% less), above that each point is worth less and the cut never reaches 100% (75 def = about 70%, 100 = about 82%).
- **Resist:** same curve as defense, for MAGICAL damage. Heroes have 7 stats in use (mana is off).
- **Weak/resist per enemy:** each enemy lists `weak` and `resists` damage types in JSON (weak = +50% damage, resists = -50%). A list can also name a whole category ("physical", "magical").
- **Damage-type bonuses** (e.g. +15% dark) also boost damage over time of that type (the bonus is baked into the DoT when it is applied). `validateDamageTypes()` checks every damage type named in the JSON (run at start, and in `damage-types.test.mjs`).
- **Melee vs ranged:** melee attacks CAN hit flying enemies but miss 15% of the time (`meleeVsFlyingMissPercent` in combat.json; user: was 40%, it made fights drag on). Ranged has no penalty. No formation rules yet.
- **Evasion:** % chance to fully dodge a hit of any type, capped at 70% (`caps.evasion`).
- **Crit:** % chance to deal 2x damage, capped at 100% (`caps.crit`).
- **Attack efficiency:** attack speed. 100 = 1 attack per second.
- **Attack timing:** a basic attack STARTS (`attackStart` event: attacker, target, `hitInMs`) and LANDS later (`attack` event). Delay = combat.json `attackTiming` per side (`hero` / `enemy`: `swingMs` 220 for melee, `rangedHitMs` 260 for arrows; 0 = instant). The walk to the target is NOT part of the delay (see MOVEMENT): a melee fighter only starts its swing once it stands next to its target. If an arrow's target dies meanwhile it bends AT ONCE to a new target (`attackRetarget`, at least `retargetMinMs` 200); a melee swing whose target dies is lost (`attackCancel` 'noTarget') and the fighter stays ready and walks to its next target. If the attacker is stunned/frozen/feared/knocked back meanwhile the attack is cancelled (`attackCancel`). Skills and item hits keep the small lunge (only from a fighter's own spot). The delay doesn't change attack speed. Tests: `attack-timing.test.mjs`, `movement-edge.test.mjs`.
- **No mana (for now):** mana is switched off. Skills run on COOLDOWN ONLY. The `mana` stat/growth in heroes.json, `manaCost` in skills.json and the mana rules in combat.json are dormant (nothing reads them).
- **Hero toughness (2026-10-19, user):** every hero's defense and resist (after levels, before items) is +10% (combat.json `heroDefense.percent`). **Death save:** once per RUN per hero, the first hit that would kill only does 50% (`deathSave.damagePercent`); if that is still enough the hero dies anyway (save used up either way). Runs before Phoenix Feather. Event `deathSave` { unit, survived }. `BattleState.deathSave()`, test `death-save.test.mjs`; other mechanic tests use `plainRules()` (util.mjs) = both off. Effect (smart curve, 360 runs): median floor 32 -> 37, alive after floor 20 78% -> 84%.
- **Hero in danger look (2026-10-19, user):** combat.json `heroDanger`. Below 30% health a soft red glow rises from the bottom edge of the screen under that hero's slot (stronger the lower, slow pulse; `BattleScene.drawDangerGlow`). A hero that falls (`heroFell`): white flash, tips over towards the middle of the screen, lies a moment, then POOF (`heroPoof`, smoke cloud) and the body is replaced by a pixel tombstone (`makeGrave`, `v.grave`, hidden with the body after the door walk); '<NAME> FELL' in red over it and its glow column flares. The death save has no look yet.
- **Healing:** each hero heals 25% of max health after a win (bigger after bosses).
- **Squad of 3:** a run has 3 heroes (`squad.json`: Knight, Rogue, Archer; the other 7 heroes stay in `heroes.json` as the bench; `pickSquad()` in `src/systems/party.js`). No boxes behind the hero slots any more; name, perk line and skill squares sit under each hero.
- **Hero perks (party buffs, decided by the user one by one):** each hero has a `perk` in heroes.json (`mods` shaped like item effects: `stat` + `percent`/`flat`, or a `special` + `percent` such as skillDamage/regen/dotDamage; plus `growth` per level). While that hero is ALIVE the whole party gets it (the hero too). Knight Stalwart +8 defense and +5% max health, Rogue Shadow Step +10 evasion, Archer Marksman +8% attack speed, Berserker Battle Fury +10% attack, Samurai Bushido +8 crit, Mage Arcane Haste +10% skill damage, Cleric Blessing +0.4% health regen per second (fights only), Necromancer Grim Resolve +8 resist, Bard Inspiration +6 evasion, Alchemist Volatile Mix +10% damage over time (`dotDamage`, applies to DoTs the hero's side puts on enemies). A dead hero's perk is lost. The perk is NOT shown in the fight any more (user's choice: no text under the skill squares); it shows on the squad screen.
- **Morale:** when heroes fall the living ones get a permanent (for the run) debuff, in combat.json `morale`: INCOMPLETE (one hero down, 3 of 3 becomes 2 alive): -10% attack, -10% attack speed, -3 defense, -3 resist. ALL ALONE (one hero left): -25% attack, -25% attack speed, -8 defense, -8 resist. Shown as a faint colour wash over every living hero (no text, no popup): light purple = INCOMPLETE, darker = ALL ALONE (`tint` / `tintAmount` in combat.json `morale`, `Character.setWash()`, `BattleScene.drawMoraleWash()`); `morale` event from `BattleState.killUnit`. Tests: `party.test.mjs`.
- **Dead heroes:** stay dead for the rest of the run. Run ends when all 3 are dead.
- **Tap-to-focus:** all heroes attack the tapped enemy until it dies (melee heroes walk to it wherever it is), then go back to auto-target. Auto-target: RANGED heroes pick a RANDOM living enemy on every attack; MELEE heroes keep their opponent (`foe`) until it dies, then take the NEAREST enemy (by lane, `BattleState.nearest()`). Single-target skills with no tapped enemy use the same pick.
- **DRAG ORDERS (user's sketch):** press a living hero during a fight and DRAG onto an enemy = ONLY that hero attacks that enemy (basic attacks and its one-target skills; a melee hero walks to it) until it dies or the floor ends; tap focus on enemies is unchanged for the rest of the squad, and an order beats tap focus for its hero (taunt/charm still win; an enemy that can't be hit right now, e.g. a burrowed Worm, is skipped until it can). Rules: `BattleState.setOrder(heroUid, enemyUid)` / `orderOf(hero)` / `hero.order` (checked first in `pickTarget()`, cleared in killUnit and spawnEnemies). Scene: heroes are interactive (`startOrderDrag()`), a red arrow follows the finger and the enemy under it gets a red ring (`drawOrders()`; small enemies stay lit a moment so a slipping finger still counts, `enemyAt()` reach 40px+), letting go elsewhere does nothing; a set order = 'On it!' + a thin orange ring under the target and a small one under the hero. Tests: `drag-orders.test.mjs`. BUG FIX at the same time: BattleScene `create()` now resets its cached drawing layers (statusG, hitG, eliteG...), on a SECOND run they were destroyed and status/hit effects silently stopped showing.
- **Levelling:** heroes gain XP from kills (`xp` per enemy in enemies.json). An enemy's XP is SPLIT between living heroes; dead heroes are frozen. Needed XP: `xpBase * xpGrowth^(level-1)` (leveling.json). Each hero has its own `growthPercent` (% of base per level: health, attack, mana) and `growthFlat` (points per level: defense, resist, evasion, crit, attackEfficiency) in heroes.json. On level-up, max health rises and current health rises by the same amount. Levels reset every run.
