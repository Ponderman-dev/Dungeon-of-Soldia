Skill name: B, floating name

Your pick: no cut-in bar. The name floats over the hero once, in CAPITALS, at one fixed size, slides up a little, stays, then fades. It never stretches.

The skill look: 3 beats

CHARGEDuring the wind-up his outline lights up blue, little specks get pulled in, a soft glow under his feet.
RELEASEHe lights up and dashes or jumps, the camera nudges in a little, a short freeze on the hit.
IMPACTBash: his shield's print flashes on the enemy, who skids back in a little dust. Challenge: he shouts, the enemies get a red "!", a blue shield pops up beside him.
