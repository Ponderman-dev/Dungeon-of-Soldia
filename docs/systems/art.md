# Pixel-art parts and animation

Character parts, hero/minion sprites, keyframe clips.
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- Every character part is a small pixel picture: `art: { palette: ["0x..."], rows: ["..01.."] }` (palette index per pixel, "." = empty), `anchor: [ax, ay]` = the turning point inside the picture (0,0 = top-left; 2.5 = middle of the 3rd pixel), `x`/`y` = the picture's middle, `w`/`h` = its size. A part WITHOUT `art` is still drawn as its old block (`w`, `h`, `color`, optional `pivotY`), now as pixels with a dark outline. Helpers: `src/systems/pixelArt.js` (tests: `pixel-art.test.mjs`).
- HERO SPRITES (done): all 10 heroes have pixel-art parts, EXACTLY 3 parts each (head, body, weapon; hats/hoods/halos/feathers/goggles are drawn into the head), about 23-24 px tall (a bit shorter than the old 28 px blocks, user's choice), weapon anchor = its grip at the body's hand. Enemies have pixel sprites too (see Enemy roster). MINIONS have simple pixel sprites too (user's OK on the proposal https://claude.ai/artifact/2BpJkQAsqqxBjnKcEibJpL; 3-5 colours, no tiny details; weak ones smaller than heroes, greater ones near hero size): Skeleton (green eyes + sword part), Rat, Wisp, Imp (fork part), Mimic (lid = `head` part that chomps), Gargoyle, Spirit Knight (head/body/sword, `alpha` 0.8), Phoenix (`wingL` part flaps). Source: `docs/proposals/minion-designs.mjs` (`toGame()`), applied with `scripts/apply-designs.mjs`; editable in the Studio (Units > Minions) like any character.
- Animation stays SMOOTH (user choice: pixel sprites, NOT pixel-step animation): `Character.parts[name]` is a pose object (x, y, angle) that tweens move; each part's image copies it every frame (`prerender`) and turns around its anchor. Hit flash = fill tint; morale wash = recoloured texture.

- KEYFRAME CLIPS (`anims` per character, made on the studio TIMELINE): `src/systems/animClips.js` (tests: `anim-clips.test.mjs`). Clips: idle (loop), attack (stretched to the swing time, or by its `hitAt`), windup (stretched to the skill's charge time, holds), skillAttack, skillSupport, walk (loop while moving), hurt (on a hit), death (holds), stunned (loop while stunned/frozen: `ch.setStunned()` from followPositions), victory (once when a floor is won: `ch.cheer()` in onWin). Priority: death > hurt > action (attack, skill, victory) > stunned > walk > idle. Tracks per part: x, y (pixels, added to the pose) and angle (degrees, turns around the anchor); keys `{ t, v, ease }`, ease = linear / ease / hold. Character plays the most important running clip (death > hurt > attack/skill > walk > idle) on top of the built-in tweens; an action WITHOUT a clip keeps the built-in motion. Battle calls `ch.hurt()` on hits, `ch.die()` on death, `ch.setWalking()` every frame.
