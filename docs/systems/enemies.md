# Enemies, floor director, elites, Monster House

Roster, enemy traits, the floor director, elites, Monster House, new enemy batches.
Moved out of CLAUDE.md (2026-10-17). Keep this file up to date when the system changes.

- **Enemy roster (new):** 15 enemies in enemies.json, all simple pixel sprites (body part + a `weapon` part for Goblin, Skeleton Archer, Bandit, Orc Brute). Floors 1-3 are fixed (dungeons.json); from floor 4 a floor entry's `enemies` (or `otherwise`) can be a NUMBER = that many random enemies from the dungeon `roster` (`{ id, from, until?, weight?, max? }`, `enemiesForFloor(dungeon, n, rng)` / `rosterFor()` / `randomEnemies()` in dungeon.js): Slime 1+, Goblin 1-40, Bat 2+, Cave Rat 4+ (the three FODDER stay on every floor), Skeleton Archer 6+, Giant Spider 8+, Bandit 10+, Fire Imp 13+, Orc Brute 16+ (max 1 per fight), Frost Wisp 19+, Puffshroom 22+, Armored Beetle 25+, Storm Sprite 28+, Dark Cultist 31+, Ghost 34+. Enemy traits: `onHit: [status spec + chance %]` (Spider poison, Bandit bleed, Imp burn, Wisp chill, Sprite shock, Cultist curse, Ghost fear; only when the hit connects) and `onDeath: { radius, color, apply }` (Puffshroom poison cloud on heroes/minions in range, `deathBurst` event, drawn as a spreading cloud). `alpha` on a def makes it see-through (Ghost). Tests: `enemy-traits.test.mjs`, `dungeon-roster.test.mjs`. The new enemies made runs somewhat harder (balance bot medians ~15-25); not tuned yet.
- **FLOOR DIRECTOR (user's choice: a true roguelike mix, but delicate; replaces the old fixed 3-4 enemies):** from floor 4 every normal floor gets a THREAT BUDGET (dungeons.json `A.director`: `budget.base` 3 + `perFloor` 0.04 per floor, swung +-`swing` 15%) spent on roster enemies by TIER (enemies.json `tier`: `fodder` Slime/Bat/Cave Rat + the new Imp Swarmling 13+ (burn on hit), Mushling 22+ (tiny poison puff on death), Bone Rat 25+ (fast), Shade Wisp 35+ (dark, flying, ranged) cost 0.4, normal 1, `strong` Orc Brute/Armored Beetle 2). A random SHAPE picks the mix (`shapes`: swarm = mostly fodder, squad = normals, heavy = a strong one first + fodder, mixed); never the same shape twice in a row, after a hard floor (swing > half) the next swing is not upwards; count `minCount` 2 to the floor's `maxCount` (4 at floor 4 ... 10 from floor 50); a full count with budget left upgrades fodder to normals. `directFloor()` / `enemiesForFloor(dungeon, n, rng, memory, enemyDefs)` in dungeon.js (memory = `BattleScene.director`, reset per run; the list has `.shape`). FODDER (combat.json `enemyTiers.fodder`, from floor 4; floors 1-3 keep the old intro fights): health x0.45, attack x0.6, floor growth x0.35 (they stay fodder deep down), XP x0.5, drawn at `drawScale` 2. CROWDS stand in ROWS (`src/systems/formation.js` `enemySpots()`, layout `enemyPerRow` 5 / `enemyRowGap` 70): up to 5 = one row like before, more = 2-3 rows, ranged at the back. `bigFightEase` is gone. Dev server only: `?floor=N` starts the run on floor N. New enemies are written by `scripts/art/add-enemies.mjs <ids>` (sprites from `docs/proposals/enemy-designs.mjs`, rules in its RULES table, adds the roster entry). Tests: `director.test.mjs`. Smart player (3/squad): median 33, mean 36, nobody past 100 (old: median 31, some runs to 400).
- **ELITES (step 3 of the enemy plan, user's choices):** from floor 10 the floor director can make enemies ELITE (dungeons.json `director.elites`: `chance` [floor, %] points 5% at 10, 33% at 40, 60% at 60, 80% from 100; `second` elite 15% at 60 to 35% at 100; `pointValue()` reads straight lines between points; `pickElites()` in dungeon.js puts indexes in `ids.elites`, `spawnEnemies` calls `makeElite()`). Only `normal` and `strong` tier enemies (user: never fodder, bosses, boss helpers, objects, rooms). Rules in combat.json `elites`, code in `src/systems/elites.js` (`eliteMethods` mixed into BattleState): health x2, attack x1.35, +10 defense/resist, XP x3, drawn 1 pixel size bigger; ONE random ELEMENT: Burning (Burn on hit, resists fire, weak ice), Frozen (Chill, resists ice, weak fire), Storm (Shock, every 4th hit arcs to a 2nd hero, resists electric, weak ice), Cursed (Curse, heals 10% of its hits, resists dark, weak holy), Toxic (Poison + a poison cloud on death, resists dark, weak fire); its hits use the element's damage type (`unit.hitType`; it still walks/shoots like its normal self: `damageType` unchanged) + ONE random TRAIT: Vampiric (lifesteal 20%), Shielded (shield 30% max health, regrows after 6s unhit, `elite.unhit` reset in dealDamage), Thorny (reflects 20% of melee), Splitting (dies into 2 smaller NON-elite copies with 25% of its max health each, `splitCopy`), Regenerating (2%/s), Unstoppable (control statuses half as long), Explosive (death burst: 120% of its attack as its element + the element's status within 100px). Name "Burning Vampiric Bandit" (pops over it in its colour when the fight starts and FOLLOWS it while it walks until it fades, `eliteName()`). `onDeath` may now be a LIST. Enemy lifesteal is not boosted by the Cleric's aura. Look (`src/systems/eliteFx.js`): a glow in the element colour behind the fighters (pulsing floor rings + rising motes) and a small gold pixel crown just left of its health bar (`BattleScene.drawElites()`). REWARD: after a fight with an elite (`state.floorHadElite`) ONE of the 3 reward cards rolls a rarity higher (`rollChoices({ bump })`, combat.json `elites.rewardBump`; legendary stays legendary); the smart bot and review bot do the same. Dev server only: `?elite=1` = every directed fight has an elite. Tests: `elites.test.mjs`. Smart player (3/squad): with elites median 29 / mean 32 / max 135, without median 34 / mean 37 / max 400.
- **MONSTER HOUSE (step 4, user's choices):** from floor 61 every FIGHT floor (not rooms) has 5% to be a Monster House (dungeons.json `director.monsterHouse`: `fromFloor`, `chance`, `count` [11, 15], `tiers` weights fodder 2 / normal 1.4 / strong 0.25 = about half fodder (user tried 1 / 1.6 / 0.4 = 1 in 3 fodder: only 38% of real floor-61 squads survived; back to the fodder mix to try on floor 61), `rewardOdds` epic 96 / legendary 4 (user: legendary was too often)); never on a boss floor or the floor before one; two can come close together (independent rolls). `monsterHouse()` in dungeon.js (checked first in `enemiesForFloor()`, the list has `monsterHouse: true` and the usual `elites`), all 11-15 enemies at once in 3 rows; `BattleState.monsterHouse` is set by `spawnEnemies`. Scene: `monsterHouseBanner()` = a huge "IT'S A / MONSTER HOUSE!" that slams in with a red flash and a shake (the elites' names come after it); the prize is `itemPick('MONSTER HOUSE CLEARED', ..., rewardOdds, bump)` instead of the normal reward (an elite in it still bumps one card). Smart bot and review bot use the same prize; deaths show as 'MONSTER HOUSE'. Dev server only: `?house=1` = every directed fight is a Monster House. Tests: `monster-house.test.mjs`. DEMO PAGE (user: play it where it really happens, not on low floors): `npm run build:house-demo` (VITE_DEMO=house) makes `house-demo-page.html`: no rooms, starts on floor 61 with a REAL squad from `src/data/demoSquad.json` (levels, items, skill pages of a smart-player run that got through floor 60 with all 3 alive; made by `node scripts/tests/make-demo-squad.mjs [floor] [squad]`; the squad screen shows it and every hero is open), and every fight floor is a Monster House (boss floors stay boss fights); published at the Monster House Demo url in the handoff (copy to the scratchpad as `monster-house-demo.html`).
