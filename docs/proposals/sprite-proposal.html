Soldia sprite proposal

Simple pixel sprites for the 10 heroes and the 12 new enemies. Heroes keep exactly 3 parts (head, body, weapon) so each part can be redrawn and animated in the Character Studio; hats, hoods and halos belong to the head.

Few coloursEach sprite uses 3 to 5 colours and a dark outline. No tiny details that vanish at game size.
Heroes read by the headSame body size for all heroes; the helmet, hood or hat and the weapon tell them apart.
Enemies read by shapeEvery enemy has its own outline: long and low, wide and flat, a dome, a bell, a floating wisp.
Colour tells elementFire red/orange, ice pale blue, electric yellow, dark purple, holy gold.
