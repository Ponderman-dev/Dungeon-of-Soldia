Pick a part, then draw.

Each square is one pixel. Drawing outside the part makes it bigger; erasing its edges shrinks it. The anchor (crosshair) is the point the part turns around: put a sword's anchor on its handle. Keys: B pencil, E eraser, L line, R rectangle, G fill, I pick colour, A anchor, M move.
