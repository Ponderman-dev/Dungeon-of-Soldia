Proposal 1 · every 20 floors

Eight bosses

One boss every 20 floors (20 to 160). Normal floors in between, as now. Each one has ONE trick that asks you to use a different part of the game: tapping a target, timing a skill, picking an element, switching targets. Bosses show a big health bar at the top and a red cast bar before their big moves. Pixel designs are drafts in the game's style (they can be redrawn in the Studio later).

Note: today most runs end around floors 15 to 30, so the first two bosses are what players will meet; the later ones become reachable as items, skill books and balancing grow. Morthul on floor 100 is the big halfway boss. After 160 the bosses could come back as harder "echo" versions.

Proposal 2 · rework, version 2

Hero kits that feel different

Each hero gets ONE signature mechanic nobody else has. New in version 2: every hero lists the items that power them and exactly how their mechanic hooks into those items, and most heroes keep a defensive tool (some overlap there is fine, as you asked: Smoke Bomb, Arcane Ward, Bone Ward and Mystery Brew stay). The removed ideas (Vanish, Time Warp, Corpse Explosion, Detonate, Last Rites) come back as skill book pages.

Proposal 3 · new reward

Skill books

A new kind of reward that changes a hero's skills for the rest of the run, so two runs with the same squad can play differently. Books use the same reward screen (sheet, cards, "Who gets it?") with one more step: choosing the page.

Reward card: SKILL BOOK›Who reads it? (hero)›Pick 1 of 2 pages›Pip under the skill square (I, II, III)

Proposal 4 · audio, version 2

Sounds: real, short and simple

No more 8-bit beeps. The style: short, soft, real recorded sounds (wood, metal, cloth, a little magic shimmer), mixed quietly so a busy fight never gets noisy. These free libraries cover everything; all of them can be used in a paid game.
